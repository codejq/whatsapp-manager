# Privacy policy — WhatsApp Manager

*Last updated: 2026-10-19*

WhatsApp Manager is a desktop app that runs entirely on your computer.

**What the app processes.** To forward messages according to your rules, the app reads the WhatsApp
messages your linked account receives. It also stores your rules and settings, a history of recent
messages and deliveries (kept for 30 days by default), and the media files WhatsApp downloads (kept for 7
days by default).

**Where data is stored.** Only on your PC, in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\WhatsAppManager`. Passwords and other secrets
are encrypted with Windows' built-in data protection (DPAPI) for your Windows user account.

**What leaves your computer.** Only:

1. WhatsApp traffic between the app and WhatsApp's servers, as with any WhatsApp linked device;
2. the WhatsApp messages and emails that **your own rules** send, through **your own** email (SMTP) account;
3. in the GitHub download version only, a check against GitHub for new versions. You can turn this off in
   Settings.

There are no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising. The developers receive nothing.

**Your control.** You can delete rules and history in the app, unlink WhatsApp at any time, and remove all
data by uninstalling and choosing to delete settings, or by deleting the folder above.

**Not affiliated with WhatsApp.** WhatsApp Manager is an independent open-source project and is not
affiliated with WhatsApp LLC or Meta Platforms, Inc.

Questions: open an issue in the project's GitHub repository.

## سياسة الخصوصية

يعمل WhatsApp Manager بالكامل على جهازك.

- **ما يعالجه التطبيق:** رسائل واتساب التي يستقبلها حسابك المربوط، لتحويلها حسب قواعدك. ويحفظ أيضاً قواعدك
  وإعداداتك وسجل الرسائل والتحويلات (30 يوماً افتراضياً) والوسائط المنزّلة (7 أيام افتراضياً).
- **مكان الحفظ:** على جهازك فقط، في `%LOCALAPPDATA%\WhatsAppManager`. كلمات المرور مشفرة بحماية ويندوز (DPAPI).
- **ما يخرج من جهازك:** اتصال واتساب، والرسائل والبريد اللذان ترسلهما قواعدك عبر حساب بريدك الخاص، والتحقق من
  الإصدارات الجديدة على GitHub (في نسخة GitHub فقط، ويمكن إيقافه).
- لا حسابات ولا إحصاءات ولا تتبع ولا إعلانات.
- التطبيق مشروع مستقل مفتوح المصدر ولا يتبع لواتساب أو ميتا.
